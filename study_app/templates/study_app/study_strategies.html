Small habits, big progress

Study strategies

Try a few simple approaches, notice what works for you, and build a study routine that feels manageable.

Four ideas to try this week

01

Active recall

Close your notes and write down what you remember. Use flashcards to test yourself, then check and correct your answers.

02

Space your revision

Return to a topic over several days instead of cramming it all at once. A short review today can make the next one easier.

03

Make the next step small

Turn “study biology” into one clear action, like “review cell diagrams for 20 minutes.” Small starts reduce friction.

04

Protect your focus

Choose one task, silence distractions for a short block, and take a real break when you finish. Consistency matters more than marathon sessions.

Put an idea into practice

Pick a subject and start with one manageable session. Your notes and study log can help you see what is working over time.
